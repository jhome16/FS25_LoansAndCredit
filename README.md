Replaces the flat base-game loan with real loans and a credit score.

Loan types:

Term loan: unsecured, highest rate, limit set by your credit score.
Equipment loan: secured by a vehicle you own, lower rate.
Mortgage: secured by farmland you own, lowest rate and longest terms.
An existing base-game loan in your savegame is moved into the mod as a term loan, and a popup lets you choose how long to repay it.

Payments are collected automatically every month. Your credit score (300-850) sets your interest rate and rises with on-time payments. Missed payments add late fees and lower your score; too many in a row and the loan defaults, the bank can seize the collateral, and you can't borrow for a while.

Settings tab: Lenient, Normal and Strict penalty presets, plus every rate, limit and penalty value. Only the host can change them in multiplayer.

Appearance tab: 13 color themes (the same as Markets And Futures), accent color and brightness, saved per player.

Save game, Quit to Main Menu and Quit to Desktop buttons in the menu header (Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Q, Ctrl+X while the menu is open).

Open the menu with Ctrl+B (change it in Controls).

Download and install
Open the latest release and download FS25_LoansAndCredit.zip. Direct link: https://github.com/jhome16/FS25_LoansAndCredit/releases/latest/download/FS25_LoansAndCredit.zip
Put the zip file, still zipped, into your mods folder:
Windows: Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods
Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/FarmingSimulator2025/mods
Start the game and enable Loans And Credit in your savegame's mod list.
Do not use the green Code > Download ZIP button. That zip has the wrong layout and the game will not load it.
